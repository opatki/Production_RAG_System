What are the best restaurants near UC Davis's campus?
Walking distance and short-drive favorites around UC Davis, organized by cuisine and atmosphere so you can pick quickly.

Quick bites / casual

Crepeville (E Street) — Large savory and sweet crepes, good coffee, reliable for breakfast and lunch; sizable patio.
Burgers & Brew (local spots like Collegiate) — Classic American burgers, fries, casual college vibe.
Chipotle / Panda Express / Blaze Pizza (2nd Street plaza) — Fast, predictable options for quick meals near campus.
Coffee & cafés

Commune — Popular local coffeehouse with generous seating, good for studying and light bites.
Black Bear Diner (Sycamore) — Hearty breakfasts and pie in a retro diner setting.
Coffee & Chocolate (Downtown Davis) — Cozy, quality espresso and pastries.
Mediterranean / Middle Eastern

Pita Pockets / Jerusalem Café — Fresh falafel, shawarma, and vegetarian-friendly plates; fast-casual and student-budget friendly.
Nomad Café (nearby) — Mediterranean-influenced small plates and warm atmosphere.
Mexican / Latin

Taqueria Los Pericos / La Valentina — Authentic, affordable tacos and burritos; popular late-night options.
Mikuni (Silos/nearby) — Sushi and Japanese-influenced rolls; lively bar scene, good for groups.
Italian / Pizza

Mikuni (again for broader menu) — Sushi-focused but many locations include pizza/Italian-style options.
Pizza Guys / In-and-Out (local pizza joints) — Several local pizza counters serving thin-crust and personal pies.
Farm-to-table / upscale casual

The Mustard Seed — Seasonal, locally sourced American cuisine; good for date nights and nicer dinners.
Ely Café & Espresso Bar — Upscale café with seasonal plates and quality coffee (weekend brunches popular).
Vegetarian / Vegan-friendly

Veggie-centric stalls in the Davis co-op and multiple vegetarian options at Commune and local taquerias; Davis has strong farm-to-fork and vegan offerings, including dedicated vegetarian cafés downtown.
Late-night eats

Taquerias near central campus — Many remain open late and are student staples.
Food trucks and campus late-night programs — Check current campus listings for rotating vendors.
Delivery-friendly chains and local favorites

DoorDash/Grubhub/Local delivery options serve most of the above plus regional chains; many local restaurants participate for campus delivery.
Tips for choosing

For fast study sessions: Commune, Crepeville, coffee shops with Wi‑Fi.
For groups/night out: Mikuni (large menu, bar), The Mustard Seed (reservations recommended).
For authentic and cheap: local taquerias and Middle Eastern counters around campus.
For farm-to-table and seasonal produce: prioritize downtown Davis restaurants and weekday farmer’s-market-influenced menus.
Note: restaurant hours and menus change seasonally; for reservations or current hours consult the restaurant’s website or major delivery platforms.

What kinds of restaurants are you looking for?

Boba - For quantity, I’d go to Open Rice Kitchen. For quality I’d go to Teabo or MandRo. For both, I’d go to Sharetea or Brick Toast Cafe. Boba is it’s own category because there’s so many good places for it.

Coffee - hands down, it’s gonna be Dutch Bros. Their 911 is perfect for a long study session.

Ice Cream/Frozen Yogurt - For quantity, it’ll be Cultive Yogurt. For quality, Cream. Always always always Cream.

Fast food/Sandwiches - Zia’s has some pretty good sandwiches, and a great selection. Panera is a quintessential classic anywhere. Noah’s Bagels has pretty good bagels and sandwiches too.

Indian - Nothing at all in the town of Davis, but Preethi’s has good chai and a decent buffet. Dixon’s got a fantastic Punjabi Dhaba (also the restaurant’s name). I will never sink to call Raja’s Tandoor actual Indian food. They call butter chicken “tikka masala.” TIKKA MASALA. My Indian eyes cry.

Vietnamese - Pho King 4 (lol, I know how that’s pronounced), fantastic pho and pretty good spring rolls. Nice Thai tea as well, though a lot of boba places have better Thai tea.

Middle Eastern/Mediterranean - Sam’s Mediterranean Cuisine, amazing amazing amazing.

Nepalese - maybe KetMoRee, but I haven’t gone there because a lot of people mistakenly call it Indian (I’m patriotic about my food).

Mexican - nothing. Literally, nothing. I’m from Southern California, so I have relatively medium standards for Mexican food. NOTHING here satisfies it. For example, they have a Korean BBQ burrito in the taqueria here. I have no idea what that is and I’m scared to try. But if you do want to go ahead and try it out, the place is called Taqueria Davis.
