Discover New Tastes. Savor Familiar Ones.
At Latitude Restaurant, we bring the world to you. Come and explore our destination dining experience with an array of global cuisines that are inspired. We share our love of all cultures through food with local ingredients and a unique menu crafted with passion and care.
When you dine with us, you can be educated on new flavors while being reminded of ones that taste of home. We have customizable options to easily accommodate your allergies or dietary preferences. When in doubt, just ask us!
Aggie Cash Plans
are welcome—and encouraged—along with credit cards. Pay at the door and come enjoy as much as you care to eat. So stop by, explore the world of unique flavors with us and have a dining experience like no other.

Location
Latitude is located at 175 Bioletti Way, north of Tercero Hall Drive and south of Hutchison Drive, and near the Tercero residence halls, Giedt Hall, Surge III, Life Sciences and the Science Lecture Hall.
Enter the Latitude market on the north side of the building and the Latitude restaurant on the south side of the building.

Food Allergy Alert
Please be advised that
our dining facilities are not certified allergy-free or certified gluten-free facilities, as other dishes served may contain major allergens
. Our team makes every effort to identify ingredients that cause allergic reactions, ensure accurate labeling, and mitigate the risk for cross-contact. Additionally, manufacturers of commercial foods may change their formulation or substitute an ingredient with minimal notice. Please ensure you carefully read menu signage posted during your visit. If you have concerns about allergens, please communicate your needs with a dining manager. Residents may also consider applying to our
Residential Dining Dietary Support Program
.
