Restaurant Menu
Make the Sage Choice
We are your neighborhood destination for your meal and grocery needs with a market, restaurant and coffee shop all in one location! With a large, open indoor and outdoor seating area and a laid-back atmosphere, we are the ideal spot for studying and hanging out while enjoying a meal or drink. The market provides well-stocked grocery and convenience items that consistently incorporate customer feedback to have what you need in stock. The restaurant offers a late-night dinner option that is Latin-inspired, fresh, tasty and nutritious, and the coffee shop offers premium coffee, tea and pastries.
With late hours and a convenient location, you don’t need to travel far for your grocery and meal needs. We even have weekend and evening events like Bingo Night! So stay tuned for what’s upcoming. Quickly drop in for your grocery needs, or stay awhile and enjoy a meal with friends.
Host Social Events
Interested in hosting social events with us? We’re looking for student organizations and clubs to host fun, engaging and lively events at Sage Street Market | Cafe. Some examples of events we offer are trivia nights, open mic nights, game nights, video game tournaments, watch parties and more! We would love to collaborate with your group and experience the energy you bring to our space.

CalFresh is accepted here.
Visit the CalFresh webpage
to learn more about CalFresh (SNAP/EBT), including eligibility, items that may be purchased, and participating campus locations.
We gladly accept EBT at Silo Market; however, IFC Sushi and Boba is operated by a third party and is not eligible for EBT payment.
CalFresh benefits cannot be used to purchase hot food or Peet's Coffee.
If you need assistance, please visit
Aggie Compass Basic Needs Center.
